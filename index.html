Weather inputs are whole-day values (the day's highest warning, total rainfall, average wind), which are only fully known after the day ends. The models describe how common delays were under these conditions in 2021–2025; they are not a live forecast for a specific flight. Most delays come from causes not in the data, such as late inbound aircraft and air-traffic limits. September 2021 – 2022 falls in the quarantine period, when traffic was a fifth of 2025 levels.
